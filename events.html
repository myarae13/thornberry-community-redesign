Year After Year

Our Seasonal Traditions

The rhythms of the year at Thornberry Creek — events neighbors look forward to every season.

Spring

Welcome Walk & Greenspace Tidy

Neighbors greet new arrivals and freshen up the common areas as the course comes back to life.

Summer

Family Flamingo Party

Our signature warm-weather gathering — food, lawn games, and good company at a neighborhood cul-de-sac.

Late Summer

Community Meeting

The association gathers to plan, share updates, and welcome resident input at the Oneida clubhouse.

Fall

Garage Sale & Foliage Walk

A community-wide sale and an autumn stroll-and-cleanup as the maples turn brilliant red and gold.

Winter

Holiday Lights Stroll

Lit streets, warm drinks, and neighbors closing out the year together in the cold December air.
