Year After Year

The Events We Run

Our goal is to promote events that help create a welcoming environment where neighbors meet neighbors and develop friendships.

Annual

The Tailgate Party

Our tailgate party benefits the Hobart Police and Fire Departments.

Annual

The Golf Outing

Organized by the association's event planning chair, alongside our philanthropic efforts.

Annual

Flamingo Parties

Hosted by resident volunteers. Hosts are needed each year.

Annual

Child-Friendly Events

Santa & Kids, Bike Safety Day with ice cream, and the Earth Day clean-up project. Over the years many young families have become residents, prompting more family events.

Annual

The Holiday Party

Organized and coordinated for association members.

Every two years

The Rummage Sale

Coordinated every two years, in odd numbered years.

Lend a Hand

Community activities need more than a chairperson.

Each year we need volunteers to aid with mailings, staff sign-in tables, welcome guests, host our Flamingo Parties, coordinate the Rummage Sale, and assist as needed.
