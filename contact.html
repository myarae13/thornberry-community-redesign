Ways to Get Involved

Every neighbor has something to offer. Here are a few easy ways to start.

Volunteer

Help plan an event, join a cleanup, or take on a board role. Even a few hours a season makes a real difference.

Attend a Meeting

Community meetings are open to all residents — the next is August 12 at the Oneida clubhouse. Come share your ideas.

Share News

Got a milestone, a thank-you, or a heads-up for neighbors? Send it our way and we'll help spread the word.
