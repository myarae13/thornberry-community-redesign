Our Story

Neighbors looking after a place we love.

The Thornberry Creek Community Association (TCCA) is an all-volunteer organization made up of the residents of Thornberry Creek Estates.

We are a family-oriented neighborhood of more than 200 single-family homes wrapped around one of Wisconsin's most celebrated golf courses — the Thornberry Creek at Oneida. Tree-lined streets, manicured greens, and quiet lakes make it one of the most sought-after addresses just outside Green Bay.

The association exists for a simple reason: to keep it that way. Through seasonal gatherings, shared standards, and an active line of communication, we help neighbors stay connected and our community stay beautiful — season after season.

Our Purpose

What the Association Does

Four ways we work to support residents and protect the character of the neighborhood.

Bring Neighbors Together

Organizing seasonal traditions — the Flamingo Party, garage sale, foliage walk, and holiday gatherings — that turn neighbors into friends.

Protect Our Character

Upholding the shared standards and guiding principles that keep Thornberry Creek beautiful and protect every family's investment.

Keep Residents Informed

Sharing news, meeting updates, and local information through our newsletter, website, and the TCCA Facebook group.

Beautify Shared Spaces

Caring for the common greenspaces, entrances, and creekside areas that everyone enjoys — and pitching in on seasonal cleanups.

What Guides Us

Our Guiding Principles

The simple values behind everything the association does.

"To promote a safe and beautiful place to live, with a spirit of community."

Safe

Looking out for one another, sharing important contacts, and keeping our streets welcoming and secure for every family.

Beautiful

Caring for our homes and shared spaces so the neighborhood stays as picturesque as the course it surrounds.

Connected

Creating easy, friendly ways for neighbors to meet, help each other, and feel at home here.
