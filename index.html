A Day in the Life

A great place to live, and to meet your neighbors.

Thornberry Creek Estates is a family-friendly community located just outside Green Bay, Wisconsin. It is a premier residential community with over 200 single family homes adjacent to the Thornberry Creek at Oneida Golf Course. Thornberry Creek Estates is a great place to live, a great place to enjoy outdoor activities, and a great place to meet neighbors and raise a family.

Frequently Asked Questions

The essentials for residents and prospective neighbors alike.

What is the Thornberry Creek Community Association?
The TCCA is a volunteer organization made up of residents of Thornberry Creek. Our mission is to "Promote a safe and beautiful place to live with a spirit of community." TCCA consists of all Thornberry Creek Estate additions, Barnard Woods, Silverthorn Estates, and all independent parcels within the four entrances.
What is the speed limit in the neighborhood?
The speed limit in Thornberry Creek is 25 mph. All vehicles, including golf carts, should be driven safely by adults holding a valid driver's license. Our streets are public and fall under the jurisdiction of the Village of Hobart. To report a speeding vehicle, call the non-emergency number, 920-391-7470, with the license plate number. For emergencies, dial 911.
When does the board meet?
Meetings are held on the second Wednesday of each month at 6:30 pm at the Thornberry Creek at Oneida Clubhouse. TCCA members are welcome. The board typically does not meet in July or January.
What are the rules on dogs, garbage bins, and fences?
Dogs should be walked on a leash and are not allowed to run at-large. Dog stations are available throughout the neighborhood, and you should always pick up after your dog (Village of Hobart Municipal Code, Chapter 102). Garbage bins should be stored out of sight (Chapter 248, Article 22). Only decorative metal, wrought iron, or aluminum fencing may be used in backyards, for the protection of a swimming pool or where special considerations are appropriate. See our Guiding Principles for the full list.
Can I walk or ride on the golf course?
Thornberry Creek at Oneida Golf Course is private property. Residents should refrain from walking, riding, or skiing on the paths or course.
Who do I contact with a question?
Email us anytime at info@thornberrycreekcommunity.com, or see the Important Contacts page for local services and government numbers.
Stay Connected

How we keep residents informed.

We use several methods to communicate with our members and residents: letters and postcards mailed through the US Postal Service, such as the Annual Report and membership drive; flyers delivered to homes; this website; and the TCCA Facebook group, where you will find updates on upcoming events and notices for the community.
