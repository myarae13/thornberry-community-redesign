A Day in the Life

Where the fairways, lakes, and forest meet home.

Thornberry Creek Estates is a family-oriented community of more than 200 single-family homes wrapped around one of Wisconsin's most celebrated golf courses. Tree-lined streets, manicured greens, and quiet lakes make it one of the most sought-after addresses just outside Green Bay — and our volunteer association works year-round to keep it that way.

Frequently Asked Questions

The essentials for residents and prospective neighbors alike.

What is the Thornberry Creek Community Association?
The TCCA is a volunteer organization made up of residents of Thornberry Creek. We promote a safe and beautiful place to live, organize neighborhood events, share local information, and look after the shared character of the community.
Are there dues or assessments?
As a volunteer association, any voluntary dues or contributions go directly toward neighborhood events, beautification, and shared expenses. For current details, please contact the board.
How do I get involved or attend meetings?
Everyone is welcome. Join us at our community meetings — the next is August 12 at the Thornberry Creek at Oneida Clubhouse — come to a seasonal event, or join the TCCA Facebook group to connect with neighbors.
Do I need approval to make exterior changes to my home?
Our community is guided by shared standards that protect everyone's investment. Before making significant exterior changes, please review our Guiding Principles and reach out to the association if you have questions.
How do I stay up to date on news and events?
Sign up for our newsletter, follow the Facebook group, and check this website's Events and News sections for the latest announcements.
Who do I contact with a question?
Email us anytime at info@thornberrycreekcommunity.com, or see the Important Contacts page for board members and local services.
