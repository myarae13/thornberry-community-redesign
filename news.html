Fall Color Walk & Cleanup Returns October 2

Join neighbors for our annual autumn stroll and a friendly tidy-up of the common greenspaces along the creek. It's our favorite way to enjoy the season's color while keeping Thornberry Creek looking its best.

We'll meet at the creekside common, provide coffee and cider, and wrap up by midday. Bring gloves if you have them — the association supplies bags and tools.
