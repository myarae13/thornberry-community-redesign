Guiding Principles

The shared standards and covenants that protect our community's character — and every neighbor's investment.

Home/About/Guiding Principles

Thornberry Creek is beautiful because we tend to it together. Our guiding principles are the shared standards that keep the neighborhood welcoming, well-kept, and a sound place to own a home.

These principles aren't about red tape — they're a simple agreement among neighbors. By looking after our own homes and being mindful of the shared character of the community, each of us helps protect the value and beauty that drew us here in the first place.

Our north star: to promote a safe and beautiful place to live, with a spirit of community. Every standard below flows from that single idea.

Homes & Property

Keeping each home and yard well-maintained is the foundation of a beautiful neighborhood. Residents are asked to:

  • Keep lawns mowed, edged, and free of weeds through the growing season, and tidy of leaves and debris in the fall.
  • Maintain the exterior of the home — siding, trim, roofs, and drives — in good repair.
  • Store trash and recycling containers out of view between collection days.
  • Keep landscaping trimmed so sidewalks, sightlines, and neighboring views stay clear.

Exterior Changes

Significant exterior projects affect the look and feel of the whole street, so a little coordination goes a long way. Before starting major work — additions, fences, sheds, major landscaping, exterior color changes, or similar — please review the community's standards and reach out to the association with any questions.

Planning a project? When in doubt, ask first. A quick email to the board can save time later and keeps the neighborhood consistent and beautiful for everyone.

Signage & Parking

  • Keep temporary signage (real estate, contractor, event) modest and remove it promptly once it's no longer needed.
  • Park in driveways and garages where possible; avoid long-term street parking that crowds the neighborhood.
  • Keep recreational vehicles, trailers, and boats stored appropriately and out of long-term street view.

Pets & Shared Spaces

  • Leash pets on walks and always clean up after them — especially along the creek and common greenspaces.
  • Treat the common areas as everyone's front yard: enjoy them, and help keep them litter-free.
  • Be mindful of noise, especially in the early morning and evening hours.

Good-Neighbor Courtesy

Beyond any written standard, the simplest principle is the most important one: be a good neighbor. Wave hello, look out for one another, welcome new arrivals, and bring concerns to each other (or the association) with kindness. That spirit is what makes Thornberry Creek more than just a beautiful place — it makes it home.

At a Glance

Seasonal Courtesy Reminders

The everyday basics that keep our streets looking their best, season to season.

🌷 Spring

Tidy winter debris, refresh mulch and beds, and repair anything the snow roughed up. A great time for that exterior project — ask first.

☀️ Summer

Keep lawns mowed and watered, trim landscaping back from sidewalks, and store bins and toys out of view.

🍂 Fall

Stay ahead of falling leaves, clear gutters and drains, and join the foliage walk cleanup along the creek.

Good to Know

Questions About the Standards

The essentials residents ask most.

Do I need approval to make exterior changes?
For significant exterior changes — additions, fences, sheds, major landscaping, or color changes — please review the community's standards and reach out to the association before you begin. Routine maintenance and like-for-like repairs generally don't require any sign-off.
Are there dues or assessments?
As a volunteer association, any voluntary dues or contributions go directly toward neighborhood events, beautification, and shared expenses. For current details, please contact the board.
What happens if a property isn't being maintained?
We always start with a friendly, neighborly conversation. Most concerns are simple misunderstandings or a neighbor who could use a hand. If you have a concern, reach out to the association and we'll help address it respectfully.
Where can I get a copy of the full covenants?
Email info@thornberrycreekcommunity.com and a board member will share the current community standards and any associated documents for your home.

Still have a question? The board is happy to help. Reach us at info@thornberrycreekcommunity.com or see Important Contacts.
